# The third version's first prototype, set aside

The first prototype of the book's chapters 1 and 2 (_Un punto en la pantalla_, _¿Está delante?_), read by the author
on 2026-11-18 and set aside because it began past the basics ([tutorial-research.md](../tutorial-research.md) §4).
Its pages are kept here as text; they are not built. Their code still compiles where it lives:

- the project's two steps, in `site/src/course/material/` (a track of their own, so they do not mix with the book's);
- the figures _front_, _shadow_ and _cone_, in `site/src/lessons/material/`.

Where the material goes in the book ([tutorial-v3.md](../tutorial-v3.md) §7): the projection, its worked example and
the pixel compared with three.js's, to chapter 7, _Un cubo_; the dot product's derivations, to chapter 6, _Flechas_; the front figure, to chapter 10,
_Caras_; the cone, to chapter 41, _Ver_.

The earlier tutorials left the site on 2026-11-18 ([decision 0030](../decisions/0030-the-book.md)). Their pages' text
is in the repository at commit `7707772`; their code stays where it was (`site/src/lessons/stage-*`, `parte-*`,
`notation`, `typescript`; `site/src/course/parte-*`), as material.
